PsijRunner

class ase2sprkkr.execution.psij.PsijRunner(executor, reader, spec, callback=None, *, output_listener=None, start=False, job=None, attached=False, run_id=None)[source]

One job managed by a PSI/J executor.

PSI/J scheduler executors redirect stdout/stderr to files. The runner follows those files while the job is active and feeds the same asynchronous output reader that is used by LocalRunner.
